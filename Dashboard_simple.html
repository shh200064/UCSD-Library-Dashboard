Featured Story · Special Collections

Students Using Special Collections for Original Research

Students in a history seminar collaborated with librarians to explore archival materials in Special Collections. Through guided instruction and research consultations, students analyzed primary sources and produced original research papers based on historical documents — many handling rare materials for the first time.
