Impact Story · Academic Success

The library strengthens student academic success by integrating research support into the curriculum. Librarians partner with faculty to design assignments and teach students how to find, evaluate, and use information — developing critical thinking and research skills for complex coursework, capstones, and graduate-level work.

Impact Story · Reducing Financial Barriers

By replacing costly textbooks with library-provided and open resources, the library reduces the financial burden on students while ensuring equitable access to required course materials — removing a critical barrier to academic success for students of all financial backgrounds.

Featured Story · Special Collections & Archival Research

Students Using Special Collections for Original Research

Students in a history seminar collaborated with librarians to explore archival materials in Special Collections. Through guided instruction and individual research consultations, students analyzed primary sources and produced original research papers based on rare historical documents — many handling these materials for the very first time.
